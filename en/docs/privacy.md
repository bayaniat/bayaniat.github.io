# Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

This application is designed to operate primarily on your device while minimizing the collection of personal information.

---

# Information We Collect

The application currently does **not** collect:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Location
- Photos
- Files
- Contacts
- User-generated data

---

# Local Data Storage

All data entered into the application is stored locally on the user's device.

This data is not transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

---

# Crash Reports

If the application encounters an unexpected error, technical diagnostic information may be automatically sent through a crash reporting service to help improve the stability of the application.

This information may include:

- Device model
- Operating system version
- Application version
- Crash logs
- Technical diagnostic information related to the error

Crash reports do **not** include user-generated content stored within the application.

---

# Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

The using of analytics to understand basic usage of the app, including device type, approximate country, theme preference, and technical errors. analytics not used to identify you or collect information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

# Data Security

Since user data is stored locally on the device, users are responsible for protecting their own devices and maintaining backups if necessary.

Although reasonable security measures are taken, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
